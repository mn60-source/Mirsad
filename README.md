Mirsad | Landslide Risk Prediction System

Mirsad is an AI-powered platform designed to predict and monitor landslide risks in southwestern Saudi Arabia.

The platform integrates geological and topographical data to generate landslide susceptibility maps, identify high-risk areas, and support early warning decision-making.

Key Features

• Risk Mapping: Interactive maps displaying landslide risk levels across different areas.
• AI-Based Prediction: Uses data-driven models to assess landslide susceptibility.
• Early Warning System: Suggests alerts for areas classified as high risk or above.
• Data Transparency: Provides information about data sources, model evaluation, and known limitations.
• Human-in-the-Loop: Ensures that alerts require human approval before adoption.

Project Objectives

• Support early detection of potential landslide hazards.
• Help protect lives and infrastructure.
• Provide data-driven insights for risk assessment.
• Support informed decision-making through interactive visualizations.

Project Status

Academic project – Under Development.

Note: The current warning workflow is simulated, and alerts require human approval. The platform is designed as a decision-support tool, not a replacement for official authorities or geological experts.
